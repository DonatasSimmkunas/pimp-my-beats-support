Support

Need a hand with your next track? Start with the help below or contact us by email.

Record with a beat

Connect headphones, choose a built-in beat, and tap Record with beat. The app reminds you to wear headphones before recording. The beat plays through your headphones while your vocal take is recorded.

Find and play saved ideas

Your recordings and mixes are stored in the app’s local library. Open My Ideas to play them. Keep the app installed to keep your local projects, and export WAV files you want to keep elsewhere.

Lyrics and voice sketches

Lyrics can be rendered as a pitched, rhythmic talk-sing sketch using voices available on your iPhone. This feature is a voice sketch, not a realistic AI singer or a voice-cloning service.

Subscriptions

If you purchase a Pro subscription, you can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Contact

Email info@donrona.lt. Please include your app version and a short description of the issue. Do not email passwords, verification codes, or private audio unless you have chosen to share it.
