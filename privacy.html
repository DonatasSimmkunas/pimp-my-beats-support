PIMP MY BEATS

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Pimp My Beats is an on-device music sketch studio for iPhone. The app does not require an account. This policy explains how information is handled by the current app.

Audio, lyrics, and projects

When you choose to record, the app uses the microphone to capture your audio. When you import audio, iOS gives the app access to the file you select. Recordings, imported audio, lyrics, project details, generated mixes, and your free-mix count are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not upload this content to a developer server or a cloud music-generation service.

Your device backup settings may include app data in an iPhone or iCloud backup. When you export or share audio, iOS sends the file to the destination you select; that destination’s privacy practices then apply.

Microphone and file access

The microphone is used only when you start a recording. Audio import uses the iOS file picker so you choose which file to provide. You can manage microphone access in iPhone Settings.

Storage and deletion

You can delete ideas and their saved versions from My Ideas. Removing the app removes its local library from the device. Exported copies and existing device backups are separate. Deleting the app does not cancel an active subscription.

Purchases

Apple processes any in-app purchase through the App Store. The app checks Apple’s verified purchase status to enable Pro features. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Analytics, advertising, and social examples

The current app includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. Discover sample tracks and heart controls are local examples; there is no public creator feed, voting service, or account-based community in this version.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer, Donatas Simkunas, at info@donrona.lt.
